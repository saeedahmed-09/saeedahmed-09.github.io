Pond's Product Photography.

6 AI-generated shots, each with the exact prompt used to create it.

Prompt
Create an ultra-photorealistic premium commercial advertising photograph using the supplied **PRODUCT REFERENCE IMAGE** as the primary source of truth. ## PRODUCT CONSISTENCY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY Use the supplied original product image as the **EXACT PRODUCT REFERENCE**. Recreate the same physical product shown in the uploaded reference with maximum visual consistency. Preserve exactly: * Product shape and dimensions * Packaging proportions and geometry * Cap, lid, pump, nozzle or closure * Logo and branding * Label placement * Typography and readable wording * Product color * Material and finish * Transparency / translucency * Surface texture * Seams and packaging details * Overall visual identity **DO NOT redesign the product. DO NOT replace the packaging. DO NOT change its shape, color, branding or proportions. DO NOT invent packaging elements. DO NOT remove or add text.** The uploaded product must remain the **hero product** and must look like the same real physical product professionally photographed in a studio. ## PRODUCT-BASED CREATIVE DIRECTION Analyze the uploaded product and automatically create a creative concept that is **directly related to that specific product**. The styling, environment, props, model styling, clothing, makeup, colors, textures and overall mood must be intelligently selected based on: * Product category * Product colors * Packaging design * Brand personality * Product ingredients or visual concept, when identifiable * Product texture * Target audience * Luxury / minimal / playful / elegant / fresh / premium character of the product **Do not use fixed props or colors from this example unless they naturally belong to the uploaded product.** For example, if the uploaded product is: * Skincare → use elegant skincare textures, botanical elements, cream/gel details or beauty-studio styling * Lip care → use glossy lip textures, soft beauty elements and subtle candy/fruit-inspired details * Perfume → use flowers, fragrance ingredients, elegant fabric, glass, smoke or luxury materials * Haircare → use silky textures, botanical ingredients, water or hair-related styling * Makeup → use beauty tools, cosmetic textures and sophisticated vanity styling * Food/beverage → use realistic ingredients and serving elements related to the product * Fashion/accessories → create an environment and model styling matching the product's aesthetic * Technology → use modern architectural surfaces, controlled reflections and contemporary styling The final scene must feel **specifically designed for the uploaded product**, not like a generic template. ## MODEL & PRODUCT INTERACTION Include an elegant, attractive young adult model whose appearance and styling naturally complement the uploaded product. The model should interact naturally with the product, for example: * Holding the product beside the face * Presenting it toward the camera * Holding it naturally at chest/shoulder level * Applying or demonstrating the product when appropriate * Looking toward the product or camera naturally Choose the interaction based on the **type and physical form of the uploaded product**. The model's clothing, hairstyle and makeup should be selected to complement the product's own visual identity. Avoid styling that competes with the packaging. The product must remain clearly visible and recognizable. ## COMPOSITION Vertical **4:5 portrait composition**. Create a premium beauty/lifestyle advertising composition. The model and product should be the two strongest visual focal points. Position the product so that: * It is clearly visible * The front/important branding faces the camera whenever physically possible * The label remains unobstructed * The product is large enough to identify * Hands and fingers do not cover important branding Use natural depth between foreground, model/product and background. Composition should feel intentional, balanced and suitable for a premium social-media advertising campaign. ## BACKGROUND & SET Create a sophisticated studio environment specifically matched to the uploaded product. Automatically derive the main background color palette from the product packaging and branding. Use subtle elements such as: * Elegant architectural forms * Soft gradients * Premium tabletop/surfaces * Glass or acrylic elements * Product-related ingredients * Relevant textures * Minimal decorative props * Soft environmental shadows Keep the set clean and luxurious. **No random props. Every visible styling element should have a logical relationship with the product.** ## LIGHTING Use professional high-end commercial advertising lighting. Large soft key light from an appropriate direction, gentle fill light and subtle separation/backlight. Create: * Natural skin highlights * Realistic skin texture * Controlled product reflections * Accurate material response * Soft contact shadows * Subtle rim lighting * Realistic highlights and reflections * Natural depth and dimensionality Lighting should be customized to the material of the uploaded product. Glossy products should have controlled specular highlights. Matte products should have soft diffused reflections. Glass or translucent products should show realistic transparency and internal light behavior. Metallic products should have physically accurate reflections. ## CAMERA LOOK High-end commercial photography captured on a professional full-frame camera. Approximately **85mm portrait/product lens**, natural perspective, f/2.8 look, shallow but controlled depth of field. The product label and important product details must remain sharp and readable. The model's face should also remain naturally sharp. Background should gradually fall into a soft realistic blur. Avoid excessive artificial bokeh. ## REALISM Maximum photorealism. The image must look like an actual professional advertising photograph, **not AI-generated artwork or a 3D render**. Ensure: * Natural human anatomy * Realistic hands and fingers * Correct finger count * Natural facial proportions * Authentic hair strands * Realistic skin pores and fine texture * Natural fabric texture * Physically accurate product materials * Realistic reflections * Correct shadows * Correct perspective * Believable product scale * Realistic contact between hands and product Avoid plastic-looking skin, distorted hands, warped packaging, fake reflections, floating objects or artificial CGI appearance. ## COLOR DIRECTION Build the entire color palette around the **uploaded product's actual colors**. Do not force a predefined pink, purple, blue or any other palette. The product's original color must remain accurate. Use complementary colors in the: * Background * Model's clothing * Makeup * Props * Surface * Lighting Create a sophisticated monochromatic or complementary palette that makes the product stand out. ## BRAND & LABEL ACCURACY The product's existing logo, label, typography and readable text must remain visually faithful to the reference. Do not generate alternative branding. Do not replace letters. Do not create fake logos. Do not invent product claims or additional text. ## FINAL CREATIVE RESULT The final image should feel like a **custom-made luxury advertising campaign created specifically for the uploaded product**. It should combine: **Exact Product + Relevant Model + Product-Specific Theme + Premium Set Design + Professional Lighting + Photorealistic Materials + Commercial Beauty Photography** The uploaded product is always the **source of truth**. Everything else — model styling, outfit, background, props, colors, textures, lighting mood and environment — should automatically adapt to the product. ### OUTPUT **1080 × 1350 px** **4:5 vertical** Ultra-detailed Ultra-photorealistic Premium commercial advertising quality Luxury brand campaign aesthetic Professional beauty/product photography

Place the product prominently in the center or slightly above the visual midpoint. Keep the packaging fully visible and ensure the logo and important label information remain unobstructed.
