B&B Body Wash Product Photography.

Premium AI-generated beauty photography for B&B Rice Glow Deep-Cleansing Body Wash.

Prompt
Create an ultra-photorealistic premium beauty advertising campaign image using the supplied B&B Body Wash product reference as the exact product source of truth. PRODUCT CONSISTENCY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY Recreate the exact same B&B Rice Glow Deep-Cleansing Body Wash packaging shown in the reference. Preserve the bottle shape, dimensions, pump, proportions, label placement, logo, typography, readable wording, colors, material, finish and all visible packaging details. Do not redesign, simplify, replace or reinterpret the bottle. Do not change the packaging shape, pump, branding, label, colors or proportions. Do not invent additional product text. CREATIVE DIRECTION Create a sophisticated premium skincare/body-care campaign with a clean, fresh and luxurious beauty aesthetic. Use the product as the hero while naturally incorporating rice-inspired visual storytelling and soft self-care elements. Include an elegant young adult female beauty model with realistic skin texture, natural facial proportions and polished but understated makeup. Her styling should complement the soft neutral packaging and premium body-care positioning. The model may interact naturally with the body wash or present it toward the camera, while keeping the bottle label clearly visible and unobstructed. COMPOSITION Vertical 4:5 portrait composition, optimized for Instagram and premium social-media advertising. Use layered editorial composition with the model and product as the strongest focal points. Maintain clean negative space and a refined visual hierarchy. Use subtle foreground/background depth and premium beauty-campaign framing. Keep the product large enough to clearly recognize the packaging and branding. SETTING & PROPS Create a warm, clean, sophisticated bathroom/beauty-studio atmosphere inspired by rice, gentle cleansing and self-care. Use tasteful neutral textures, soft fabric/towel details, subtle rice grains or rice-inspired natural elements, and premium cosmetic surfaces only where they support the story. Avoid clutter and unrelated props. Every visible element should feel intentionally selected for a luxury body-care campaign. LIGHTING Use soft high-end commercial beauty lighting with a large diffused key light, gentle fill and subtle separation light. Create natural skin highlights, realistic bottle reflections, soft contact shadows and believable material response. CAMERA Professional full-frame commercial photography look, approximately 70–100mm lens perspective, natural proportions, controlled depth of field and sharp product branding. Keep the model's face and hero product naturally crisp while allowing the background to fall into a realistic soft blur. REALISM Maximum photorealism. Realistic human anatomy, hands and fingers, authentic skin pores, natural hair strands, believable fabric texture, physically accurate plastic/bottle material, realistic reflections and contact shadows. Avoid CGI appearance, plastic-looking skin, distorted hands, warped packaging, fake labels, floating objects, excessive bokeh or unrealistic reflections. FINAL OUTPUT 1080 × 1350 px 4:5 vertical Ultra-photorealistic premium commercial beauty photography Luxury body-care campaign aesthetic Clean, elegant, product-focused art direction MOST IMPORTANT: The uploaded B&B Body Wash product remains visually unchanged and is the primary source of truth. Adapt the model, environment, props, lighting and color palette around the actual product.
