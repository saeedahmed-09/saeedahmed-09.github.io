Prompt
Create an ultra-photorealistic **premium product campaign photograph** using the supplied product reference. ### PRODUCT CONSISTENCY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY Use the supplied original product image as the **primary, exact and highest-priority product reference**. Recreate the exact supplied product with maximum visual consistency, preserving: * Exact product shape and proportions * Exact physical dimensions * Exact packaging geometry * Exact cap, lid, pump, nozzle or closure * Exact label placement and orientation * Exact logo and visible typography * Exact readable product information * Exact colors * Exact surface finish * Exact material properties * Exact transparency or translucency where applicable Preserve the original product's physical construction and recognizable identity. **DO NOT redesign, replace, simplify or reinterpret the product.** Do not change the packaging color, alter the logo, invent packaging components, remove important label information, add fake text, distort typography or transform the product into another product. The final product must look like a **real physical product photographed professionally**, with accurate geometry, realistic materials, natural reflections, subtle surface texture, realistic seams and believable contact shadows. ### SCENE — PRODUCT-CENTERED ARCHITECTURAL CAMPAIGN Place the exact supplied product upright as the **hero subject** on a premium architectural pedestal or display surface. Create a sophisticated layered composition using: * One primary pedestal or platform * One optional lower circular/geometric platform * Subtle foreground elements * Elegant product-relevant props The pedestal shape, material and color must be **adapted to the actual product**. Do not automatically use lilac, pink or cylindrical platforms. For example, depending on the product, the display may use: * Matte stone * Polished acrylic * Frosted glass * Marble * Ceramic * Brushed metal * Natural wood * Glossy resin * Minimal sculptural surfaces Choose whichever material best complements the supplied product. ### PRODUCT-ADAPTIVE STYLING Build the complete campaign around the **actual visual identity of the supplied product**. Analyze: * Product category * Packaging color * Packaging material * Brand personality * Product finish * Luxury level * Texture * Intended use * Natural, clinical, modern, playful, premium or minimal aesthetic Then automatically determine the: **pedestal + background + props + surface + color palette + lighting mood + composition** The product itself should control the art direction. ### FOREGROUND DETAILS Add a small amount of **product-relevant visual detail** in the lower foreground only when appropriate. Examples: * Product texture smear * Cream or gel detail * Ingredient element * Botanical detail * Water droplets * Powder particles * Fabric * Fruit or natural ingredient * Crystalline material * Small decorative brand-relevant objects These elements must feel naturally connected to the supplied product. **Never add random props simply to fill space.** If the product has no suitable texture or ingredient representation, keep the foreground clean and minimal. ### COMPOSITION Vertical **4:5 premium commercial composition**. Create a clean, sophisticated and visually balanced product portrait. The hero product should occupy approximately the **central 30–40% of the frame**, depending on its actual dimensions. Keep: * Product perfectly stable * Product clearly readable * Front-facing or most commercially attractive angle * Important branding unobstructed * Strong visual hierarchy * Generous negative space The architectural platforms should create depth and hierarchy without competing with the product. Use symmetry when it suits the product; otherwise use subtle asymmetry for a more editorial composition. ### BACKGROUND — ADAPT TO PRODUCT Create a seamless premium studio background based on the product's own visual identity. Use colors derived from the supplied product rather than a fixed pink/purple palette. Possible treatments include: * Soft monochromatic gradient * Neutral luxury studio * Product-matched color field * Warm natural environment * Cool modern environment * Dark dramatic luxury setting * Bright clinical beauty setting * Organic botanical atmosphere Add subtle architectural shapes, curved panels, translucent surfaces or abstract forms only when they complement the product. Keep the background visually clean and softly separated from the hero product. ### PROPS — MINIMAL AND PRODUCT RELEVANT Use only a few carefully selected props that strengthen the product story. Props must match the product's: * Color palette * Material * Category * Brand aesthetic * Intended use Do not use generic decorative objects. Every secondary element should support the hero product rather than distract from it. ### LIGHTING Use high-end commercial product photography lighting tailored specifically to the supplied product. Use: * Large soft directional key light * Gentle fill * Controlled rear rim light * Subtle edge highlights * Natural contact shadows * Realistic surface reflections * Controlled contrast Adapt the lighting to the product's material. For example: Glossy packaging → refined controlled specular highlights. Matte packaging → soft gradients and subtle edge definition. Glass → realistic refraction and reflections. Metal → crisp but controlled highlights. Transparent packaging → believable internal light transmission. ### CAMERA LOOK Professional **85–100mm product photography lens aesthetic**. Eye-level to slightly elevated camera position depending on the product shape. Use controlled depth of field so: * Product packaging remains exceptionally sharp * Logo and important label details remain readable * Foreground has subtle optical falloff * Background gradually becomes soft Natural photographic perspective with premium editorial depth. ### REALISM LOCK The final image must look like a **real high-end commercial product photograph**, not a 3D render. Prioritize: * Exact product geometry * Accurate packaging proportions * Realistic material behavior * Natural contact shadows * Authentic reflections * Physically correct transparency * Realistic surface imperfections * Natural perspective * Believable pedestal contact * Professional studio lighting * Premium editorial polish Avoid: * Warped packaging * Fake labels * Incorrect typography * Invented product details * Floating objects * Unrealistic reflections * Excessive glow * Plastic-looking surfaces * Over-stylized CGI * Random props * Unnecessary visual clutter ### OUTPUT **1080 × 1350 px** **4:5 vertical** **Ultra-photorealistic** **High-end commercial product campaign photography** **Clean, sophisticated and premium editorial aesthetic** ### FINAL ADAPTATION RULE — MOST IMPORTANT **Do not force the supplied product into the example's pink/lilac skincare theme.** The uploaded product is the source of truth. Automatically adapt the **pedestal design, materials, background, colors, props, texture details, lighting, atmosphere and overall art direction** to the specific product being supplied. The final image should feel like a **custom luxury advertising campaign created specifically for that exact product**, while maintaining 100% visual priority on the original product identity and packaging.
